# EU–EU municipal cooperation (complement to this study)

Research briefing (2026-09-18). **Not** UA–EU twinning, **not** Law 1508-VII
МСС, **not** Law 3668-IX. How EU municipalities cooperate *with each other* —
domestically and across internal EU borders.

Product rule (already locked in
[internal/interreg-proposal.md](../internal/interreg-proposal.md)): this is
**context**, not a new matcher. Do not fold into v7 `score`. Do not set
`known: true`. Full EU↔EU matching remains Phase 3.

Companion canvas (open beside chat): `eu-imc.canvas.tsx`.

## Why this sits next to our research

Our corpus already covers three *Ukrainian* layers:

| Layer | Instrument | What it is |
|-------|------------|------------|
| Domestic IMC | Law 1508-VII | Five legal forms; registry + PIN |
| UA–EU people | SKEW / AFCCRE / C4C | Sister-city / jumelage |
| UA–EU project | Interreg NEXT + keep.eu | Mostly CBC with PL/HU/SK/RO |
| UA–EU registered | Law 3668-IX | International territorial agreements |

EU municipalities do the same three things **among themselves**, at much larger
scale, under 27 national IMC laws plus EU territorial-cooperation instruments.
The analogue of «з ким · про що · навіщо» is therefore not one EU product — it
is three stacks that EU practitioners also keep separate.

Central finding for this study:

> In the EU, most *everyday* municipal cooperation is **domestic service
> sharing** under national law (water, waste, fire, back-office). Cross-border
> work is a thinner, more funded layer (Interreg / EGTC / Euroregions).
> Twinning is a third, people-to-people layer. Conflating them is the same
> mistake as treating SKEW as Law 1508.

OECD (2026) treats IMC as the pragmatic alternative to mergers when
municipalities are small: EU average municipal size ~5 300 inhabitants; ~29% of
EU municipalities have fewer than 2 000 residents (Czechia, France, Slovakia
>80%). That is the same scale problem Ukraine solved by amalgamation into ОТГ
— then reopened as МСС among the amalgamated units.

## Three stacks (keep them apart)

```
1. Domestic IMC          2. Cross-border EU–EU         3. Soft / people
   national law             Madrid Conv. + EU law         CEMR twinning
   EPCI / Zweckverband      Euroregion → EGTC             Europe for Citizens
   unione / związek         Interreg A (CBC)              URBACT / city nets
   mancomunidad / CIM       Interreg B/C (policy)         LEADER LAG (rural)
```

Stack 1 is the true EU analogue of Law 1508. Stack 2 is the analogue of our
Interreg + 3668 corridor. Stack 3 is the analogue of SKEW/AFCCRE — except
inside the EU it is older (post-1945 FR–DE) and much denser.

Hulst & Van Montfort’s four types still hold: quasi-regional governments,
planning forums, service-delivery organisations, service-delivery agreements.
Most of the volume sits in the last two (operational). That matches our
`зручний сусід` track — geography + existing network — not Goals-cosine.

## Stack 1 — domestic IMC (the bulk)

There is **no EU-wide IMC registry**. Counts are national, incomparable, and
often mix active bodies with dormant registrations (Poland: 323 registered
unions vs 168 that filed 2022 accounts). Treat the numbers below as order of
magnitude, not a league table.

### Two policy styles

| Style | What the state does | Typical countries | UA analogue |
|-------|---------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Coverage / mandated** | Almost every commune must sit in a multi-purpose body; sometimes own-source tax | France EPCI; Portugal CIM (via EU-fund steering); Hungary joint offices for <2 000 inh. | Closer to агломерація / mandatory service districts — **not** Law 1508 |
| **Voluntary menu** | Constitution guarantees the right to associate; several legal forms; take-up uneven | Poland, Czechia, Spain, Italy, Germany, Romania, Slovakia | **Law 1508** (five forms, voluntary, registry) |

France is the extreme: IMC *replaced* amalgamation. As of 1 Jan 2026, DGCL
counts **1 252 EPCI à fiscalité propre** (21 métropoles, 14 communautés
urbaines, 230 d’agglomération, 987 de communes); only four mainland communes
sit outside. Syndicats (single-/multi-purpose, no own tax) keep falling:
**8 322** on 1 Jan 2025 (−3.6% y/y). A commune belongs to exactly one EPCI
but may join many syndicats.

Germany is the other classic: *Zweckverband* (special-purpose public-law
association) for water, waste, transport, hospitals, plus contractual
delegation and joint admin offices. OECD/UCLG (2022): **4 606**
*Gemeindeverbände* of all kinds in 2021 (not all are Zweckverbände). Rules
are Land-level, not federal.

**Italy 2025 caveat:** Decree-Law 202/2024 (*Milleproroghe*) abolished the
Delrio-era duty for comuni <5 000 to jointly manage “fundamental functions”.
Unioni remain the preferred *voluntary* vehicle; they are no longer a legal
stick.

### What EU domestic IMC is usually *about*

Same sectors our PIN already sees, with more depth on capital-intensive
networks:

- **Utilities:** water, wastewater, waste, sometimes energy / district heat
- **Safety:** fire, rescue, civil protection, local police
- **Back-office:** accounting, payroll, IT, procurement, building control
  (Ireland’s LGMA shared-services; Poland CUW; Slovenia JMA)
- **Transport / planning** at metropolitan or NUTS-3 scale
- **Not usually:** schools and elderly care in Nordics (too politically
  salient) — contrast Ukraine, where education/health/social ~20% of titled
  МСС (DECIDE)

OECD (2026) enabling conditions map onto our product almost 1:1: incremental
trust path; data/monitoring; sector fit (capital networks + cross-boundary
development). Partner search remains hard even where the legal menu is
mature — the DOBRE 30% finding is not Ukraine-specific.

## Stack 2 — cross-border EU–EU

Legal ladder, oldest first:

1. **Council of Europe Madrid Outline Convention** (1980) — states undertake
   to enable transfrontier cooperation of territorial authorities. Not EU law.
   Model agreements in the annex; domestic powers still govern.
2. **Nordic Convention** (1977, DK/FI/NO/SE) — municipalities may cooperate
   across Nordic borders as they do at home, typically under **private law**
   (public-law joint entities do not travel well across the border).
3. **Euroregions** (from late 1950s, EUREGIO 1958) — usually associations or
   parallel NGOs on each side. AEBR (~100 members) is the lobby, not a
   census. Many Euroregions are information/lobby hubs more than service
   operators (Perkmann).
4. **EGTC** — Regulation 1082/2006, revised 1302/2013. EU legal personality
   for a grouping with members in ≥2 Member States (or 1 MS + neighbouring
   third country under conditions). Assembly + director; **cannot** receive
   regulatory/police powers. CoR keeps the register: **93** EGTCs listed,
   **7** closed (EP fact sheet, Apr 2026). New 2025 example: BOTILA
   (Bodrog–Tisza–Latorca, HU–SK, registered 29.8.2025).
5. **Interreg** (ETC) — the **funded project** layer. An EGTC may *implement*
   an Interreg programme or project; most Interreg partnerships never become
   an EGTC.

### Interreg scale (this is EU–EU, not our UA NEXT slice)

Regulation (EU) 2021/1059: **€8.05 bn** ERDF (2018 prices) for 2021–2027.

| Strand | Share | € bn | What municipalities actually do |
|--------|-------|------|----------------------------------|
| **A — CBC** (internal land/maritime) | 72.2% | 5.81 | Neighbour-to-neighbour projects: mobility, environment, health, tourism, labour market |
| **B — transnational** | 18.2% | 1.47 | Macro-regions (Danube, Baltic, Alpine, Med…) — more regions than communes |
| **C — interregional** | 6.1% | 0.49 | Interreg Europe, URBACT, ESPON, Interact — policy learning, not a service |
| **D — outermost** | 3.5% | 0.28 | Outermost regions |

ingest):

| | 2021–2027 overall | keep.eu | All periods overall |
|--|-------------------|---------|---------------------|
| Projects | **6 637** | 5 523 (83%) | **36 882** / 32 310 in keep.eu (88%) |
| of which internal CBC | 4 116 | 3 445 | 23 982 |
| Partnerships | 37 863 | 29 751 | 182 172 |
| Programmes | 84 | 76 with project rows | 357 / 347 |

Honest caveat we already apply on the UA side: many “partners” are regions,
universities, hospitals, agencies — not the municipal council. The same
`organisation_type == Local public authority` filter is required for any
EU–EU municipal claim.

Interreg Europe 2021–2027: €379 m ERDF; public authorities 80% co-financing;
**calls for 2021–2027 are closed** (budget committed). Ukraine is now
eligible as a partner country on that programme — that is *policy learning*,
not CBC service sharing.

### What EGTC is (and is not)

- **Is:** a durable legal wrapper so a hospital, bus line, or park can have
  one budget and one director across a border (examples in the EP fact sheet:
  cross-border hospitals, transport, development projects; also the
  non-contiguous European Urban Knowledge Network).
- **Is not:** an IMC type, a grant, or a substitute for Interreg rules.
  Parliament (2018, 2022) still regrets under-use: fear of competence
  transfer + ignorance of who may join.

Closest UA analogue: a *future* joint body under Law 1508 form 5, or a
3668-registered international agreement that actually operates something —
not a keep.eu project row.

## Stack 3 — twinning and rural CLLD

**CEMR twinning** (formal, unlimited in time, community involvement):

- 2006 CEMR survey: **>17 000** links in 35 countries
- 2010 CEMR census (still the last full count): **~34 000** partnerships in
  37 countries. Top stocks: France 6 776, Germany 6 277, Poland 3 508, Italy
  2 755, UK 2 059. FR–DE corridor **2 281** (includes softer “contacts”)
- 2011 Rybnik congress talking point: “40 000” — treat as advocacy, not a
  new census
- CEMR 2023 *Analysis of Twinning*: 315 responses, **not** a new stock
  count. >2/3 of respondents active in the last two years; typical activity
  is people-to-people, culture, youth — not utilities

This is the EU-internal version of our SKEW/AFCCRE layer. Same product
discipline: node highlight / weak social-capital signal, never `known: true`.

**LEADER / CLLD** (CAP 2023–27): **~2 678** Local Action Groups planned;
**€5.06 bn** EAFRD (7.7% of Pillar 2). A LAG is a public–private local
partnership implementing a local development strategy — municipalities sit
*in* it, they do not *become* it. Useful analogue for rural multi-party
clusters (our tourism / canyon cases), not for ЦНАП sharing.

**City networks:** Eurocities, CEMR, UBC, Energy Cities, Covenant of Mayors
— agenda clubs, not IMC contracts.

## Map onto Law 1508 and our signals

| Law 1508 form | Closest EU-internal device | Which of our signals would find it |
|---------------|----------------------------|------------------------------------|
| 1. Delegation of tasks | DE *öffentlich-rechtliche Vereinbarung*; PL *porozumienie*; FI/SE contract; HU joint office | Geo / neighbour; almost never Goals text |
| 2. Joint project (UA “light” ~57–61%) | Interreg project; convenzione; handshake; LEADER project | Explicit-ask + funder overlap; title cosine if both have a strategy |
| 3. Joint financing of existing entity | Host-municipality / *centrumgemeente*; Swedish *gemensam nämnd* | Geo + named asset |
| 4. Joint enterprise / utility | Zweckverband; spółka; intercommunale; IDA; *kuntayhtymä* | Geo / shared infrastructure; HydroBASINS as *context* for water |
| 5. Joint management body | EPCI; Unione; CIM; EGTC; metropolitan union | Matching finds *candidates*; Provan & Kenis picks the governance mode |

OECD 2026 ladder (handshake → single-purpose → multi-purpose → NAO-like)
is already in [REFERENCES.md](../REFERENCES.md). EU evidence reinforces the
split we ship: **operational** cooperation is next-door and under-described
in strategy text; **thematic** clusters (tourism, energy transition, digital)
are where NLP has comparative advantage — and where Interreg B/C and city
networks live.

Tkachuk’s mechanism travels: trust between heads, clear «навіщо»,
associations as warm intros (CEMR / national LGAs, not cold email). Frozen
elections are the *Ukrainian* twist — EU municipalities still run regular
local cycles, so *fragile heads* is the default there.

## What this does *not* change in the product

Copied from the Interreg decision draft, restated for this briefing:

1. Do not build EU↔EU cosine until there is a broker who will use it and a
   validation registry (keep.eu LPA-filtered is the only EU-wide candidate;
   national IMC registers are 27 separate plumbing jobs).
2. Interreg NEXT + twinning on the UA side remain the shovel-ready bridge
   ([ua-eu-twinning.md](./ua-eu-twinning.md), `yarn interreg`).
3. If we ever port the engine, **pick one instrument first** — Interreg A
   for border hromadas, or a single national IMC register (Poland *związki*
   is the closest legal cousin of Law 1508).
4. France EPCI / Portugal CIM are **not** the port target: they are
   mandatory coverage systems, not partner-search markets.
